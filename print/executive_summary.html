Trinity Air Link Transportation SystemExecutive Summary · March 2026

The Vision: Fort Worth's Transportation Future Starts Here

The Trinity Air Link is a proposed public-private transformation of the historic Texas & Pacific Warehouse in downtown Fort Worth into America's first fully integrated multi-modal transportation hub — combining commercial eVTOL aircraft, Level 4 autonomous vehicles, a 120,000 SF innovation center, and a restored historic landmark into a single connected facility.


The preferred model is capital-light and operator-neutral: certified operators carry vehicle capex while Trinity Air Link monetizes infrastructure access, charging, dispatch, passenger processing, data services, and operator agreements.

Air Mobility Platform

Rooftop vertiport access, pad fees, charging, passenger processing, dispatch, and data services for certified eVTOL operators. Aircraft ownership is optional, not required for launch.

Ground Mobility Platform

Level 4 AV bay access, curb-use fees, charging/energy services, passenger facility fees, software, data services, and operator service-level agreements.

Innovation Center

120,000 SF of Class A technology workspace in a nationally recognized historic building supporting transportation tech, aerospace, AI, robotics, and logistics tenants.

The Asset: T&P Warehouse

The 8-story Texas & Pacific Warehouse is an architecturally significant historic property adjacent to Fort Worth's Amtrak station, TEXRail terminus, and Trinity Metro bus hub — one of the only sites in the region with the scale and intermodal connectivity required for a true transportation hub. Federal Historic Tax Credits reduce effective renovation cost.

The Regulatory Moment

The FAA eVTOL Integration Pilot Program selected Texas DOT + Archer Aviation as a national site. The program creates a formal pathway for operational integration planning and regulator coordination while FAA certification and safety requirements remain fully in force. Fort Worth can position as the western DFW vertiport anchor.
