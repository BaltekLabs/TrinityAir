Section 02 · Technology Categories

Partnership Opportunities by Technology Domain

eVTOL Aircraft Systems Active Procurement 2027–2028

Initial fleet of 10–15 aircraft (~$1.3M/unit manufacturing cost), scaling to 25–40 units by Year 5. Requirements: 4–6 passenger capacity, 15–60 mile range, electric propulsion, autonomous capability with pilot override. Partners: Joby S4, Archer Midnight, and compatible alternatives. Opportunities include aircraft supply/leasing, maintenance partnerships, pilot training, fleet management integration.

Autonomous Vehicle Fleet Active Procurement 2027–2028

Level 4+ autonomous platform for first/last-mile connectivity. Fleet of 50–100 vehicles by Year 5. Requirements: Electric powertrain, fast charging, 4–8 passenger capacity, weather adaptability, hub traffic management integration. Waymo, May Mobility, and comparable platforms. Opportunities: fleet supply agreements, software updates, charging infrastructure, maintenance partnerships, data sharing.

AI Traffic Management & Routing Platform Development Partnership 2026–2027

Multi-modal coordination system integrating air and ground transportation in real time. Requirements: Real-time routing/scheduling optimization, weather integration, safety monitoring, passenger interface, emergency response protocols. Opportunities: Software platform development, AI algorithm optimization, data analytics, cybersecurity integration, mobile app development.

Charging & Energy Infrastructure Infrastructure Build 2027

High-speed EV charging for both eVTOL aircraft and AV ground fleet. Integrated energy management and grid coordination. Requirements: VTOL landing pad support systems, rapid charging capability, energy management, renewable integration. Opportunities: Infrastructure design, energy systems, building automation, telecommunications.

Innovation Center Technology Platform Long-term Partnership

120,000 sq ft of innovation space (floors 2–7) as a technology development hub. Anchor tenant opportunities for eVTOL/AV research, AI development, urban mobility startups. Lease rates: $29–37/sq ft (Fort Worth Class A). Access to live operational data from hub systems for R&D.
