Why Conventional Solutions Fail

Highway expansion induces new demand within 5–7 years, restoring congestion to prior levels — a well-documented phenomenon in Texas Transportation Institute data. The I-35 expansion is a case study in this failure mode.

Rail expansion is politically constrained, capital-intensive ($150–300M/mile for light rail), and optimized for fixed corridors rather than the distributed, point-to-point demand patterns of an 8,000-square-mile sprawling metroplex.

Less than 1% of DFW workers use public transit. Approximately 70% drive alone. The problem requires a fundamentally different approach — a new dimension: altitude.

Commercial Operations 2026
Archer Aviation — Midnight eVTOL

Holds FAA Part 135 Air Carrier, Part 145 Repair Station, Part 141 Pilot Training certificates. Texas eIPP participant. 4 passengers + pilot, 150 mph, 60-mile range. Converts 60–90 min DFW car trip to 10–20 min flight. Manufacturing cost ~$1.3M/unit.

FAA Stage 4/5 — Targeting 2026 Launch
Joby Aviation — S4 eVTOL

Completed FAA Stage 4 of 5 type certification (November 2025) — first in history. Targeting first U.S. commercial passenger flights in 2026. Also a Texas eIPP participant. 4 passengers + pilot. ~$3/seat-mile target pricing.

Level 4 Autonomy — Commercially Proven
Waymo — Autonomous Vehicles

450,000+ paid rides per week across five U.S. cities including Austin, TX. Valued at $126B. Expanding to Atlanta, Miami, D.C. in 2026. 88% fewer property damage claims vs. human drivers. Proven business model at scale.

Active Policy Framework
Federal eIPP Program

White House eVTOL Integration Pilot Program enables revenue-generating passenger operations before full FAA type certification. 8 sites selected nationwide. Texas DOT selected with Archer. Operational H2 2026. Fort Worth needs vertiport infrastructure.
