Risk Management Framework Summary

Phased Approach as Risk Control: The 4-phase development structure is itself the primary risk mitigation tool. Each phase requires milestone achievement before capital commitment for the next phase — limiting maximum exposure to phase-level investment ($20–50M) rather than full project capital ($129M). Revenue diversification across 5 streams ensures no single service failure is existential.
