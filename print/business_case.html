Section 01

The Business Opportunity

The Problem

  • DFW congestion and growth are outpacing conventional road expansion.
  • Fort Worth needs a western DFW infrastructure anchor for next-generation mobility.
  • The T&P Warehouse remains a large vacant transportation-era asset.

The Solution

  • Repurpose the T&P Warehouse as a vertiport, AV access, charging, dispatch, and passenger-processing platform.
  • Use a capital-light operator access model instead of forced fleet ownership.
  • Add innovation-center, technology/data, and ancillary revenue streams.
Model discipline: The base case return is calculated before terminal value. Terminal value is shown separately as exit/refinance upside and is not counted as operating cash flow.

Investor Case

  • Capital-light base case shifts vehicle capex, maintenance, depreciation, insurance, and much operating risk to qualified operators.
  • Trinity Air Link monetizes access, charging, dispatch, passenger processing, data, service standards, and real estate.
  • Owned-fleet exposure remains optional and tactical, not the baseline launch assumption.
