Section 01 · Purpose

What We Need Reviewed

This review is intended to answer one question: are the sponsor-level cost assumptions directionally reasonable for an early phased activation of the Texas & Pacific Warehouse as a mobility infrastructure hub?

Requested feedback: please mark where assumptions look materially too low, too high, missing, or improperly categorized. We are specifically trying to avoid underestimating historic-rehab, utility, rooftop, and commissioning risk.

Working Project Assumptions

  • Historic warehouse adaptive reuse in Fort Worth, Texas.
  • Early activation is phased, not full-building repositioning.
  • Initial active program likely falls in the range of 120,000–180,000 square feet.
  • Program includes passenger-facing space, innovation-center buildout, rooftop vertiport infrastructure, AV curb/bay infrastructure, charging/power upgrades, and operations/control systems.
  • Vehicle fleet acquisition is not included as a baseline construction cost assumption.

Section 03 · Big Questions

Questions for Builder / GC Review

  1. Assuming a phased activation strategy, what is the most likely square-footage band you would regard as realistic for the current cost framing?
  2. Which category above appears most under-budgeted?
  3. Which category above appears least reliable without drawings, utility studies, or code analysis?
  4. Does the rooftop / vertiport package look materially too low once access, life safety, and structural implications are considered?
  5. Do the MEP and utility ranges reflect enough risk for a historic adaptive-reuse project of this type?
  6. Is the contingency range adequate for this stage, or should the base case carry more contingency?
  7. If you were underwriting this at concept stage, which assumptions would you mark as “cannot validate yet” versus “directionally reasonable”?
Section 04 · Requested Output

What We Want Back

  • A quick red / yellow / green reaction to each category.
  • Any category where the low or base case is clearly too aggressive.
  • Any material missing category.
  • An opinion on whether the current $107M–$113M capital-light total case is only defensible under a clearly phased activation strategy.
  • A rough “if I were sanity-checking this for a sponsor, I’d think the real first-wave band is closer to $X–$Y” view.
Important: we are not asking for a detailed estimate at this stage. We are asking for a top-level reality check so the sponsor model does not rely on construction and infrastructure assumptions that sophisticated reviewers will immediately reject.

Builder Review Use
