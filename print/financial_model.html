Capital Structure — $129M Owned-Fleet Case

Capital-Light Operator Access Case: approximately $107M–$113M total investment depending on final Phase 3 scope. The financial model retains the $129M case as the upside/control scenario while adding a preferred capital-light scenario that reduces Phase 3 vehicle capex. The $32M Phase 3 vehicle deployment remains the full-control owned-fleet case.
