Preferred Base Case: The capital-light operator access model requires approximately $107M–$113M. Certified eVTOL and qualified AV operators carry vehicle capex, pilots/drivers or autonomy operations, maintenance, insurance, and fleet financing. Trinity Air Link monetizes vertiport access, AV bays, charging, dispatch, passenger processing, data, and service standards.
