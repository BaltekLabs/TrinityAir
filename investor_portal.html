Infrastructure economics, not flying-car capex.

The preferred Trinity Air Link case is a capital-light operator access model. Certified eVTOL and qualified AV operators carry vehicle capex while Trinity Air Link monetizes vertiport access, AV bays, charging, dispatch, passenger processing, data, and service standards.
