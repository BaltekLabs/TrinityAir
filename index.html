The revised thesis

Build the missing downtown endpoint—not another airline.

Texas's advanced-mobility ecosystem is becoming real. Trinity Air Link's potential advantage is the downtown interface: a place where qualified operators can connect passengers and freight to rail, autonomous ground vehicles, parking, hospitality, and the Fort Worth core.

01

Operator access

Shared pads, charging, passenger processing, dispatch support, data services, and contracted access fees for qualified third-party operators.

02

Ground integration

A managed interchange for rail, autonomous ride-hail, shuttles, parking, curb operations, and first/last-mile connections.

03

Adaptive reuse

A phased commercial program intended to preserve a landmark while putting only financeable, technically feasible space into service.

Preferred site

The T&P Warehouse is a hypothesis to test.

The preferred property is the warehouse at 401 W Lancaster Avenue, adjacent to downtown rail access. It is strategically distinctive, but Trinity Air does not currently represent that it controls the property.

Secure an option, lease framework, or other enforceable diligence right.
Confirm parcel boundaries, title, access, building area, condition, and historic constraints.
Test structure, fire/life safety, utilities, charging, airspace, noise, emergency response, and vertiport geometry.
Maintain a ground-level or alternate downtown-site configuration if rooftop operations fail.
Current disclosure: site control, operator commitments, construction budget, incentives, and FAA operating authority have not been established in public project evidence.

Near-term roadmap

A 90-day evidence sprint.

Now · Days 0–30

Control and scope

Resolve property interest, commission capped diligence scopes, establish the claims register, and open working-level conversations with the city, TxDOT, operators, and regional planners.

Next · Days 31–60

Test and price

Complete preliminary site, structure, utility, airspace, fire, and operating studies. Rebuild unit economics from operator-access fees rather than gross transportation fares.

Then · Days 61–90

Decide

Proceed only if site control, a viable minimum configuration, commercial traction, financeability, and a named public/regulatory path are all present. Otherwise pivot or pause.
