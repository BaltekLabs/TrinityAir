## WHO GETS THE WHITE PAPER

The white paper is a **long-form technical and policy document** — 20+ sections covering market analysis, technical architecture, regulatory pathway, and full financial model. Send it to audiences who will actually read it and need depth to make decisions:

- **Texas DOT eIPP team** — needs technical/regulatory depth to evaluate partnership
- **FAA contacts** — regulatory pathway section is directly relevant
- **Reilly Brennan (Trucks VC)** — transportation-specialist investor who will do real diligence
- **Blackstone / KKR Infrastructure** — institutional investors requiring comprehensive documentation
- **Joby Aviation / Archer Aviation technical teams** — the architecture and integration sections
- **Michael Morris, NCTCOG** — regional transportation planner who needs full system context
- **Academic/research partners** — TCU, UTA, Texas A&M

Do **not** lead with the white paper for mayors, city managers, or generalist investors — send the Executive Summary first and offer the white paper as follow-up due diligence material.
